# MVPeters

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Simon Peters' personal website explains his work and helps prospective clients
start a project conversation by email. Hands-on product development leads the
offer, supported by AI engineering and CTO experience. Both priorities were
confirmed with Simon.

## Users

Prospective clients evaluating Simon's product development, AI engineering, and
technical leadership work. Specific industries and company stages remain open.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The launch structure is Home, About, and Contact. Home lists client work:
Shiperise, Club Gewoon, Niko, DCW, and Calabi. Each links to its client website;
Niko links to its public configurator project. Calabi also opens the existing
interim CTO video. Switch to EU and Commons Radio appear under a collapsed
Side projects disclosure. Their descriptions and ordinary external links must
be server-rendered, including while collapsed, so they remain crawlable.

The Work index and draft case studies are temporarily hidden and redirect to
the homepage project list. Keep the case-study scaffolding for later releases,
when roles, scope, collaborators, outcomes, dates, and media are confirmed.
Do not expose placeholders or illustrative interfaces in the launch version.

The development branch is `codex/mvpeters-dev`; its Vercel preview is the review
surface. Simon has asked to launch the simplified version; production deploys
through the reviewed pull request into `main`.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve MVPeters' current style, logo, personal English voice, light/dark themes,
and pragmatic kite/spacecraft philosophy.

Copy should stay close to Simon's original writing: direct, personal, technically
specific, and a little nerdy. Reuse his own introduction and contact language.
Avoid agency taglines, generic sales promises, and invented personal philosophy.
Publication is on hold while Simon reviews the revised copy; update the preview
without merging the release pull request.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing homepage, CV, testimonials, logo, and interim CTO video are real
materials. Commons Radio has a public website and real iPhone/iPad screenshots.
Other case-study media in this concept is illustrative and must be labeled.
Do not invent client testimonials, metrics, exact contributions, or outcomes.
